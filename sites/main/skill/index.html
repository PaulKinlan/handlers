Check your site with an agent

Paste SKILL.md into Claude Code, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Codex, or Antigravity. It tells the agent to read your codebase, manifest, service worker, docs, and plans, check them against the patterns on Beyond the Tab, and write up which OS integrations fit your site and which ones to skip.

Read https://beyondthetab.dev/SKILL.md and check this codebase, its docs, and its plans for OS and cross-app integration opportunities. Ask me if target platforms (including ChromeOS) are unclear, then write a report of suggested next steps and what to skip.
